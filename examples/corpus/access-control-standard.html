Access Control Standard

This standard defines who can access Example Corp environments and how that access is granted, reviewed and removed.

1 Principles

Access follows least privilege. Every grant has an owner and an expiry. Shared accounts are not permitted.

The matrix below lists the default access each role receives. Anything beyond it needs an approved exception.

3 Break-glass access

Break-glass access to production write is granted through the on-call tool for a maximum of 4 hours. Every session is recorded and reviewed by the security team within 2 business days.

4 Access reviews

4.1 Frequency

Managers review their team's access every quarter. Privileged roles are reviewed every month.

4.2 Removal

Access is removed within 24 hours of a role change and within 1 hour of termination.

  • Automated deprovisioning is triggered by the HR system.
  • Manual removals are logged in the access ticket queue.
